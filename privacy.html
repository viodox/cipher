Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

CIPHER ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website ciphersignal.xyz. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

CIPHER is designed to work without requiring user accounts or personal information. Here is what we may collect:

  • Email address: Only if you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter (Daily Cipher Drop), submit a contact form, inquire about advertising, or save a price alert. We never collect email addresses without your explicit action. A price alert stores the email, the coin id, and the USD threshold. CIPHER does not send alert email until a delivery provider is connected. Ask us if you want that row deleted.
  • Usage data: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregate usage data such as page views, visit duration, and general geographic region. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.
  • Contract addresses: When you scan a token, the address is sent to honeypot.is (EVM), RugCheck (Solana), GoPlus, and DexScreener so they can return a result. Our own server proxies CoinGecko, and it also calls those scan APIs for share pages and Rug Radar. We do not keep a database of addresses you paste. Scan results may sit in a short cache (about 60–120 seconds) so repeat views and link previews don't hammer the free APIs.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you the Daily Cipher Drop newsletter if you subscribe (managed through Kit, formerly ConvertKit)
  • To respond to your contact form submissions (processed through Formspree)
  • To understand general usage patterns and improve our service through anonymous analytics
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

Third-Party Services

CIPHER integrates with the following third-party services:

  • CoinGecko API: Provides live cryptocurrency market data. Requests go through our server so the API key is not in the page. Their privacy policy is at coingecko.com/en/privacy.
  • Honeypot.is: EVM honeypot simulation.
  • RugCheck.xyz: Solana token risk reports.
  • GoPlus: Token security (mint, proxy, blacklist function, owner powers, holders).
  • DexScreener: Pair liquidity and the public boosted/latest token lists used by Rug Radar.
  • Google AdSense and Google Analytics: Ads and a single Analytics tag. Google may use cookies. See Google's advertising policies.
  • Vercel: Hosts the site and the serverless proxies.
  • Upstash or Vercel KV: When configured, this is where price-alert signups are stored (email, coin, threshold, and an unsubscribe token).
  • Resend: When configured, this sends the confirmation email and the one alert email. Each message has a one-click unsubscribe link. Until email delivery is turned on, the alert form is hidden.
  • Cloudflare: DNS for the domain, plus the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. Cloudflare is not the web host.
  • Kit (ConvertKit): Manages our email newsletter subscriptions.
  • Formspree: Processes contact form and advertising inquiry submissions.

Cookies

Google Analytics (one tag) and Google AdSense may set cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is also loaded. CIPHER's own scanner keeps the watchlist and prediction streak in localStorage, not in a cookie. You can block or clear these in your browser.

Affiliate Links

CIPHER contains affiliate links to Coinbase, Binance.US, Kraken, Crypto.com, and Robinhood. When you click these links and sign up, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Those links live in the "where to buy" section and do not change scores or scan results.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any personal information you provide. The site is served over HTTPS on Vercel. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

CIPHER is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email
  • Request deletion of any personal information we hold about you
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or Google's ad settings

Disclaimer

CIPHER is a free informational tool and does not provide financial advice. Scores are formulas and third-party scan results, not an AI model and not a recommendation. Cryptocurrency can go to zero. Always do your own research.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, you can reach us through the contact form on our homepage.
