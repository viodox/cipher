how scores work

Scanner numbers are computed in your browser from CoinGecko market data. They are not an AI model, not a smart-contract audit, and not a promise about what happens next. The header count is however many meme coins came back on the last refresh, skipping pairs with under $500 of 24h volume.

  • Hype (viral): volume relative to market cap, the size of the 24h move, whether CoinGecko currently lists the coin as trending, absolute volume, and a small boost when a tiny cap is trading more than its size. A ±3 daily jitter keeps identical inputs from stacking.
  • Mood (sentiment): starts at 50 and moves with the 24h price change. Very low volume pulls it back toward neutral. High volume relative to market cap amplifies the move.
  • Risk level: a market-structure guess. Smaller market caps, violent 24h moves, and dead volume raise it. Large caps and healthy volume lower it. It does not read the contract. Use CipherPot for that.
  • Signal Score: the same three numbers, written out as SEND IT / WAIT / NGMI, plus a line of color. No model is called.
  • CipherPot: honeypot.is on Ethereum, BSC, and Base (honeypot simulation, taxes, liquidity, holders); RugCheck on Solana; GoPlus token security on both (mintable, proxy, blacklist function, owner privileges, holder concentration). DexScreener supplies extra liquidity when the other sources don't.
  • Rug Radar: DexScreener's free boosted and latest-profile lists, then one GoPlus batch per chain. Cached about two minutes on the server. Open a row for the full scan.
  • Top Movers: a separate page at /movers ranks 24h gainers, losers, and volume relative to market cap. Each name links to a coin page such as /coin/dogecoin.
  • On-chain stocks: CoinGecko's Robinhood Chain stocks category. Those tokens are debt securities and are not offered to US persons. That is separate from the Robinhood brokerage link under where to buy.
